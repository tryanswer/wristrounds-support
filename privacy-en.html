WristRounds privacy policy

Data on your watch

WristRounds stores your routines, cue preferences, training summaries, stopwatch laps and countdown state in the app’s local storage on your watch. Editing and saving routines does not require an app account or a phone connection. The app includes no advertising or analytics service and does not operate a cloud synchronization service.

Permissions

On Apple Watch, interval training requests workout sharing permission to use the system workout lifecycle. WristRounds does not request permission to read heart rate, location or other health measurements. Countdown alerts may request notification permission. On Wear OS, active training uses a visible foreground service, notifications and a wake lock. On Huawei, local reminders request notification consent and use the system reminder permission; vibration uses the vibration permission. The Android and Huawei apps request no network, microphone, location or health-sensor permission.

System voices

If you select a system voice, WristRounds passes fixed training cues to the watch’s speech engine and stores your selection locally. It does not record your voice or request voice downloads. Available voices, audio routing and output depend on the operating system and its settings.

Purpose and contact

WristRounds is an interval timer. It does not provide medical advice or calorie estimates. For support, send your app version, watch model and the steps that led to the problem; avoid including health records or other private information. Privacy questions can be sent through the support channel. Issues are public; do not post personal or health information.

Website and support

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Our pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or account forms. GitHub may process website request information under its privacy statement. Information you voluntarily post in support issues is public and is used to respond to your request. Do not include confidential information.

Deleting local data

You can delete saved routines in the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data subject to the watch operating system’s storage and backup behavior. This app does not provide cloud synchronization.

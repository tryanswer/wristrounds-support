WristRounds support

Create a routine, preview its phases, and save it on your watch. Saved routines can be reopened for editing.

Before reporting a problem

Include your watch model, system version, app version, the steps you followed, and what you expected. Avoid sending health records or other private information.

Training cues

Available cues depend on the watch, installed system voices, your cue settings and system permissions. Silent and Do Not Disturb settings may affect output. Check your selected cue settings before starting.

Contact

For technical support, open a support issue. This is a public channel: do not include personal information, credentials or health records.
